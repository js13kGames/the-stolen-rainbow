The seven days have lost their elements. Guide a unicorn from Tuesday's fire to Monday's moon, recover all seven, and bring the rainbow back.

The Stolen Rainbow is a pixel-art bullet-hell game built around colour. Each day gives you three related colours and weapons. Shoot matching bullets to break connected chains, and destroy their emitters to cancel the attacks still to come. Watch the warning effects before each pattern unfolds.

Seven battles, seven weapon types, and a world to restore.

Controls:
- Arrow keys: move
- Shift: precise movement; focus the fan shot
- Z: shoot
- C or 1 / 2 / 3: switch weapon
- X: bomb, clear bullets, and gain brief protection
- Esc / P: pause; Q while paused: title
- Enter: start or continue after a battle
- R: retry after defeat
- M: toggle sound effects

Only the small white point at the unicorn's centre is your hitbox. Matching bullets have a white outline. Clear a battle to recover one life and one bomb.

Desktop keyboard required. All graphics and sound run locally; no external assets are loaded.
